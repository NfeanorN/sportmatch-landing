The gap in Italian sports talent discovery

Success still depends too often on location, budget, and local networks — not potential. Thousands of capable athletes slip through the pipeline every year.

Fragmentation & cost

4.7 million officially registered athletes (CONI) across 45+ federations — yet no unified digital home to showcase talent holistically. Enterprise tools like Wyscout / Hudl run €500–2,000+/year and target clubs and analysts, not grassroots athletes.

Wrong tools for athletes

LinkedIn ignores sports-specific portfolios. Instagram and TikTok bury athletic merit in algorithmic noise. Youth football alone has 700,000+ registered players (2023/24) but most never get visibility beyond their local club.

North–South & rural access

Quality academies, showcases, and top scouts cluster in the industrial North — while rural and southern athletes face extreme access barriers. Clubs still lean on costly physical scouting; Serie A/B/C budgets for scouting and transfers run into the hundreds of millions annually.

Go-to-market — three phases

Local density first: prove the model in Italy’s largest sporting hubs, then scale nationally and deepen federation partnerships.

Phase 1 — Months 1–6 · “Plant the flag”

MVP in Milan & Rome; 3–5 Serie B/C beta partners; 2–3 athlete ambassadors; 2 physical showcases; social on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts. Target: 10k athletes, 50 club accounts.

Phase 2 — Months 7–18 · “Scale the network”

Add Naples, Turin, Bologna, Florence; “SportSync Scouting Week” across five cities; B2B push to all 20 Serie B clubs (€99/mo); FIGC & FIP partnership track; creator campaigns. Target: 100k athletes, 200 clubs, 50 sponsors.

Phase 3 — Months 19–36 · “Become the standard”

Full support for five major sports verticals; premium analytics tier for Serie A; automated AI scouting reports; Series A fundraise (€2–5M) for EU expansion. Target: 500k athletes, 1k active club accounts.
